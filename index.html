Slider 1–10 sets a VVI threshold: threshold = 0.5×(n−5)+1.0, so 1 = −1.0, 5 = +1.0, 10 = +3.5. VVI scores are standard deviations from the national mean. Areas scoring above a threshold are flagged. Scores > 1.0 are considered high.

Limitations: avoidance is a preference — Compass picks the least-exposed OSRM alternative and may add up to 3 detour waypoints; it cannot hard-block areas the way Maps avoids tolls. Google Maps may re-route between waypoints differently. The Google Maps link carries the route, not the shading (shading lives in My Maps via KML). Routing uses the public OSRM demo server and geocoding uses Nominatim (fair-use limits). VVI scores: Vizient Vulnerability Index 2026 public file (no login). Areas without data are shown grey and never flagged.
